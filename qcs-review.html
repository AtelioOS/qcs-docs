ShippedWhat it means day-to-day
Accounting packThe OS keeps real double-entry books. Journal → “Post from activity” fills them from invoices, payments and received POs — safe to press any time, nothing posts twice. Statements (trial balance, P&L, balance sheet) print clean and export CSV; a Month vs QuickBooks tab exists purely for the parallel-run reconciliation. Bank statements import as CSV and tick off against recorded payments.
Tax codes on ordersNew orders carry a QuickBooks-style tax code (default S — GST 5% + PST 7%). Issued invoices itemize the tax, and the ledger splits revenue from GST/PST payable automatically. Existing invoices are untouched.
Recorded payments“Record payment” on any issued invoice — EFT, cheque, PayPal, cash, card — with a date and reference. It marks the invoice PAID, flows to the books, and shows in Bank rec for statement matching.

The flow

1
2 min · Control Centre · everyone

Open on the home page and say nothing

Let it sit on screen: outstanding balances, credit holds, needs-attention, today's schedule. Give it a beat before you talk.

Say: "This is the page you all open in the morning now. Everything below it is one click away."
2
10 min · Jura counter station hardware in the room

Check a machine in — on the iPad, for real

Best opener you have, because it's physical. Play the customer:

  • Tap through the four check-in screens on the iPad.
  • The label prints on the MUNBYN while everyone watches.
  • Hand a scanner to a tech — have them scan the label from a computer across the room. The work order opens.
Say: "Nobody typed that work order. It was in the system before the customer left the counter."
3
8 min · Service & Dispatch · Mazda + techs

Follow that same work order through

  • Open the WO you just created — call type, technician, scheduling.
  • Billing tab: line items with your real GST/PST tax codes computing live.
  • Print WO and Email WO from the popup.
  • Calendar: the month view — and point at a PM work order the system created by itself overnight.
Say: "Preventative maintenance now books itself. Nobody has to remember."
4
10 min · Sales CRM · the emotional peak

Customer 360 — the reveal

Global search a name everyone knows: Tim Hortons or The Keg. One click opens everything:

  • Account number, payment terms, balance owing, credit hold.
  • Their machines — the registry went from 3 to 9,268 last week.
  • Service history, quotes, orders.
  • QuickBooks history — invoices going back to 2009, lifetime invoiced.

Then open one machine's serial → its invoice history includes invoices from before the OS existed.

Say the number out loud: "Twenty-six years. 94,406 invoices. $75.1 million. One click."
5
8 min · ERP · Marie + Roya their hands, not yours

Money and purchasing

  • Marie: open Reports → Money. Receivables aging, open invoices. Then let her click the CSV export.
  • Roya: create a PO live — it numbers itself 4247, continuing the QuickBooks sequence.
  • Hit Print PO and hold the printout next to the old QuickBooks template. Identical is the point.
6
4 min · Quote builder · Kazuto

A quote, saved, on the pipeline

Build a short quote with per-item dealer discounts, save it, watch it appear as an opportunity on the sales pipeline with a status you can move.

7
3 min · close

Leave the OS open on screen and let people click while you wrap up.

The moment email switches on

Kazuto / Roger / whoever owns customer comms

Which mailbox do customer emails come from, and who watches the replies?

Customers will hit Reply — it needs to land somewhere staffed.

Should replies go to that shared mailbox, or bounce to the tech / rep on the job?

PM reminders: how many days before the due date? And every customer, or contract accounts only?

The window is configurable — we just need their number. 7 / 14 / 30?

Who receives the internal daily "PM work orders created" summary?

Defaults to the sending mailbox today — Mazda or Roya is probably righter.

At the counter: does the customer get an automatic "we've received your machine" email? And a "ready for pickup" one — or does someone still phone?

Quotes: does the customer approve by clicking a link, or does a person still call to confirm?

What footer goes on every OS-sent email — address, phone, any disclaimer?

Before the cost sanity check

Roya · needed to make the numbers mean anything

What counts as "cost" — the supplier invoice figure, or landed cost with freight, duty and exchange?

Where does the dealer / wholesale price come from — one price list, or a per-customer discount off retail?

Two decisions the QuickBooks import surfaced

Roya / Marie · data shape, not technical

"Z Residental Customer" is now the largest account in the system — 14,362 invoices, $8.8M, 1,063 machines. Split it into real customers, or leave the bucket?

Decides whether Customer 360 is useful at all on the residential side.

835 backfilled machines have very short serials ("8072", "0073") — real serials, or internal reference numbers?

5,589 of the 9,268 machines came through without a make/model. Worth a cleanup pass, or is serial + customer + history enough to work with?

835 old invoices across 67 customer names didn't match anyone in the CRM — likely renamed or closed accounts. Map them, or leave them?

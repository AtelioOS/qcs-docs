Definition of done

Acceptance checklist for M1

Delivery 1 is signed off when each of these is true on your real data. We run them with you in the walkthrough.

Team logs in. Each named user signs in with their own account at the agreed access level.

Catalogue is real. Machines and parts are searchable; prices and stock match your source on a spot-check.

Inventory is accurate. On-hand counts are correct for showroom and warehouse (vans as counted).

Purchasing works. Suppliers and open POs are present, and a new PO can be raised with a lead time.

Sell & invoice. A sales order can be created and turned into an invoice end to end.

Control Centre is live. KPIs and "needs attention" reflect the loaded data, not demo placeholders.

It looks like QCS. Real logo and photography on primary screens — no placeholders where the team works daily.

Handover complete. Walkthrough delivered, documentation provided, and support-portal access granted.

What could move the date

ItemHow we handle it
Data readiness is the critical pathM1's date follows the intake. We start loading the moment the first export lands and layer the rest in — we don't wait for everything.
Van stock may lagWe go live with showroom + warehouse counts and add van locations as they're counted, without blocking sign-off.
Outlook / M365 integrationDeferred to the comms sprint — not required for Delivery 1, so admin consent can come later.
Accounting migrationRuns on a separate track with Marie; QuickBooks stays the book of record until that cutover.
